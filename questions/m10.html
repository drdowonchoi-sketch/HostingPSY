In Texas schools, How often can my child be reevaluated?

Answer key

Once a child receives special education services, a reevaluation must occur at least once every three years, unless the parent and the school agree it is unnecessary. A reevaluation may not occur more than once a year unless the parent and the school agree otherwise. Within those limits, a reevaluation is conducted if the school determines more information is needed or if the parent or the child's teacher requests one. The school must make reasonable efforts to obtain parent consent for a reevaluation; if the parent does not respond, the school may proceed without consent.

Facts an answer must state: three years · once a year  ·  Governing law: IDEA §300.303
