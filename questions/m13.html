In Texas Schools, When must the ARD committee determine eligibility?

Answer key

The ARD committee must make the initial eligibility determination, and if appropriate the IEP and placement, within 30 calendar days from the date the written full individual and initial evaluation report is completed. If the 30th day falls during the summer when school is not in session, the committee must meet by the 15th school day of the following school year, unless the evaluation report indicates the student needs extended school year (ESY) services that summer, in which case the committee must meet as soon as possible after the report is completed.

Facts an answer must state: 30 calendar days · 15th school day  ·  Governing law: TAC §89.1011

Timeline rules

1. Standard timeline. The ARD committee must make its decisions on initial eligibility, and if appropriate the IEP and placement, within 30 calendar days from completion of the written FIIE report [1].

2. 30th day falls in the summer. If school is not in session, the ARD committee must meet no later than the 15th school day of the following school year [1].

3. Summer, with ESY need. If the FIIE report indicates the student needs extended school year services that summer, the ARD committee must meet as expeditiously as possible after the report is completed [1].

4. Parent copy of the report. The parent must receive a copy of the written FIIE report as soon as possible, and no later than five school days before the initial ARD meeting. If §89.1011(e)(1) applies, the deadline is June 30 [1].

5. Consent received 35 to 45 school days before the last instructional day.

  • The evaluation must be completed and the report provided to the parent by June 30 [4].
  • The ARD committee must meet no later than the 15th school day of the following school year to consider the evaluation [4].
  • TEA's timeline guidance adds a condition: the student must not have been absent three or more days between receipt of consent and the last instructional day [10].

6. Consent received fewer than 35 school days before the last instructional day. The standard 45-school-day evaluation timeline applies to the FIIE report date [1][4].

7. Student transfers mid-evaluation. The new district must coordinate with the previous district to complete the evaluation promptly, in accordance with 34 CFR §300.301(d)(2) and (e) and §300.304(c)(5). The timelines in §89.1011(d) and (g) do not apply if the new district is making sufficient progress and the parent and new district agree to a specific completion date [1].

Related timelines

  • Evaluation report deadline. The written FIIE report is due by the 45th school day after the district receives written parental consent. That period is extended by the number of school days the student was absent if the student missed three or more days [4]. The determination is made by a group of qualified professionals and the parent, who decide whether the child is a child with a disability and what the child's educational needs are.
