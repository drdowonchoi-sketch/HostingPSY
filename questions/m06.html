In Texas schools, How can parents attend the meeting remotely?

Answer key

For an ARD committee meeting, if neither parent can attend in person, the school must use other methods to ensure parent participation, such as individual or conference telephone calls or video conferencing. The meeting must be held at a time and place agreeable to the parent and the school, and the school must make reasonable efforts to find a time the parent can meet. If the school cannot convince the parent to participate, it may hold the meeting without the parent.

  • 34 CFR §300.328: The parent and the public agency may agree to use alternative means of meeting participation, such as video conferences and conference calls [17].
