Next steps

  • Validation with real users: an IRB-approved study in which practitioners and trainees use the beta on their own questions.
  • 50-question test set: a larger set, including Texas law changes from 2024–2026; full results in a forthcoming paper.
  • Ethical guidance: route each question to law, ethics, or both, and answer ethics questions from the NASP and APA codes.

Limitations

14 questions and one state. Judges are AI models; only TXPsyBOT’s flags received expert review. Public tools change often, so these answers describe October 2026. TXPsyBOT is a research prototype and is not legal advice.
